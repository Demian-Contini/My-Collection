About Me

I'm Demian. A young with a strong passion for continuous learning, movies and series, and nature, always accompanied by a good mate and music in the background.

To me, growing isn't a destination — it's a daily habit. Every story I watch, every song I listen to, every mate shared in silence teaches me something different about who I am and who I want to become.

That's why this collection exists: it's not just a list of things I like, it's a record of what shaped the way I think. A place where I keep what I've learned, so I don't forget it — and a small way to help anyone who wants to discover themselves but doesn't know where or how to start.
